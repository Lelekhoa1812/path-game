How to Play

Connect every star in order, fill every empty cell — that's it!

The Goal

  1. Start at 1, finish at the highest number on the board.
  2. Pass through the numbered stars in order (1 → 2 → 3 …).
  3. Your path must cover every empty cell exactly once. No skipped squares, no revisits.

How to Move

  • Click & hold on the 1 star, then drag to draw your path.
  • Move up, down, left, or right only — no diagonals.
  • Release when you reach the final number.
  • Made a mistake? Click any cell on your path to rewind to that point, or hit Reset.

What the Cells Mean

1
Start — purple star, where every path begins.
N
Finish — pink star, the highest number.
7
Waypoint — must be visited at exactly that step number.
Asteroid — blocked. Your path can't enter these cells.

Tips

  • The pulsing star shows your next target.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution — if you're stuck, try a different route from a recent turn.
  • Watch Filled % in the top bar to see your progress.
You win when every cell is filled and every numbered star sits on the correct step. Good luck, explorer! ✦
